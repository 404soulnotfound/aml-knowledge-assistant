# Wolfsberg Anti-Money Laundering Principles for Correspondent Banking & CDD

## Customer Due Diligence (CDD) Standards
The Wolfsberg Group emphasizes risk-based customer identification and ongoing due diligence for financial institutions.
Key principles:
1. **Risk-Based Customer Risk Assessment (CRA)**:
   Customers must be categorized into risk tiers (Low, Medium, High) based on product type, delivery channel, geographic exposure, and customer type.
2. **Enhanced Due Diligence (EDD)**:
   Mandatory for Politically Exposed Persons (PEPs), high-net-worth individuals from sensitive industries, non-resident customers, money service businesses (MSBs), and entities located in FATF black-listed or grey-listed jurisdictions (e.g., DPRK, Iran, Myanmar).
3. **Periodic CDD Reviews**:
   - High Risk: Annual review or event-driven review triggered by material changes (change in beneficial owner, negative news screening alert).
   - Medium Risk: Review every 2 to 3 years.
   - Low Risk: Review every 3 to 5 years.

## Nested Correspondent Banking & Payable-Through Accounts
Correspondent banking relationships pose unique cross-border risks:
- **Shell Banks**: Institutions must strictly refuse to enter into or continue a correspondent banking relationship with shell banks (banks with no physical presence in the jurisdiction in which they are incorporated and licensed, and which are unaffiliated with a regulated financial group).
- **Payable-Through Accounts (PTAs)**: When respondent banks offer PTAs to their own retail customers, the correspondent bank must verify that the respondent bank performs adequate CDD on the underlying account holders and can provide customer information on demand.
