# FinCEN & Egmont Group: Financial Crime Typologies and Red Flag Indicators

## Trade-Based Money Laundering (TBML)
Trade-Based Money Laundering is the process of disguising the proceeds of crime and moving value through the use of trade transactions in an attempt to legitimize their illicit origins. Common techniques include:
1. **Over-invoicing and Under-invoicing of Goods**: Misrepresenting the price of the good in the invoice to transfer value between importer and exporter.
2. **Multiple Invoicing of Goods**: Invoicing one shipment several times to justify repetitive wire transfers.
3. **Short or Over-shipping of Goods**: Shipping more or less quantity than stated on the customs declaration.
4. **Phantom Shipments**: Invoicing goods that were never manufactured or shipped.

### TBML Red Flags
- Inconsistencies between the declared value of goods on customs declarations and commercial invoices.
- Size or nature of the shipment appears inconsistent with the exporter’s or importer’s normal business profile.
- Goods shipped through high-risk transshipment hubs without apparent commercial logic.
- Payment made by third parties with no obvious economic nexus to the underlying transaction.

## Funnel Accounts & Structuring ("Smurfing")
Structuring involves breaking down large currency deposits into amounts below statutory reporting thresholds (e.g., $10,000 in the United States under CTR rules) to evade mandatory regulatory reporting.
- **Funnel Account**: An account opened at a branch in one geographic location that receives numerous cash deposits below threshold from multiple unassociated individuals, followed by immediate cash withdrawals or international transfers at a branch in a distant location or near a border.

## Shell Companies & Complex Corporate Vehicles
Illicit actors frequently exploit legal entities to obscure the beneficial owner.
### Red Flags for Opaque Legal Entities:
- Company registered in a jurisdiction known for corporate secrecy with nominee directors or bearer shares.
- The company shares an address with dozens of other unrelated companies and lacks an active web presence, physical office, or verifiable commercial operations.
- Transactions involve round-dollar amounts or rapid in-and-out pass-through wire activity with zero operational expenses (payroll, rent, utilities).
- Beneficial ownership structure has multiple layers of holding companies across multiple offshore tax havens.
